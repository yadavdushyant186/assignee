1. Abstract

This study examines the degree of pass-through efficiency from the Reserve Bank of India’s 250 bps policy repo rate hikes (commencing May 2022) to external benchmark lending rates (EBLR) tied to floating-rate residential retail loans. Utilizing quarterly balance sheet microdata from six scheduled commercial banks, we apply a vector error correction model (VECM) to estimate the speed of transmission and subsequent elasticity in housing credit absorption across Tier-1 urban nodes. Findings indicate an asymmetric pass-through elasticity coefficient of $\beta = 0.89$ within 45 days, substantially suppressing loan inquiries in the sub-₹50 Lakh affordable housing bracket by 24.3%.
